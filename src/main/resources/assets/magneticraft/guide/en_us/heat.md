# Heat

Heat is a separate network measured in kelvin. Sources, consumers, pipes, insulated pipes, and heat sinks exchange energy according to their nodes; electrical or FE power becomes heat only through a heater.

Ordinary heat pipes trade reach for environmental loss. Insulated heat pipes reduce that loss, while heat sinks deliberately dissipate heat. Use a thermometer on the relevant face to read the authoritative node temperature.

Machines below their recipe's minimum temperature pause without consuming the pending input. Unloaded network members also pause without forcing chunks to load.
