# Getting Started

Use a crushing table and the correct hammer tier for early ore processing, then build a sluice box chain with water. The guide book lists each machine's slots, ports, redstone mode, and recipe family.

Electricity, heat, and fluids do not implicitly convert into one another. Connect the correct faces and use a voltmeter or thermometer to diagnose a stopped process. Portable batteries charge and discharge in the battery box.

Advanced production uses formed multiblocks. Build every displayed layer around the controller, keep required chunks loaded, and consult the port summary before attaching automation.
