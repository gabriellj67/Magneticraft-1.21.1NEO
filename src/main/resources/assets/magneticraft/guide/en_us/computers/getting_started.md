# Getting Started

Place a computer or mining robot and interact with it to claim ownership. The editor is available only to the owner or a server operator. Select a language, enter source, then press Upload. Uploads carry a unique menu-session token and monotonically increasing sequence; stale, replayed, forged, oversized, distant, or unauthorized requests are rejected.

Shift-use a writable floppy on a programmable device to copy its program and Shell filesystem. Use the disk normally to load it. Preset media are read-only. A damaged or future-version disk fails closed instead of partially loading.

Mining robot actions use energy and historical action delays: forward movement and rotation take five ticks, backward movement and mining take ten ticks, and scanning takes one tick. Robot state, pending actions, orientation, quarry cursor, inventory, and energy survive a save-and-restart cycle.
