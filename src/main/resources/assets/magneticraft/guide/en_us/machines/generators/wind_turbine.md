# Wind Turbine

The wind turbine generates up to 200 J/t when its rotor plane and the sixteen-block space in front remain clear. Its bounded scan checks only loaded chunks.

Electrical output is available on the rear-facing native port. Generation writes directly into the rated electrical node, which applies backpressure when the network cannot accept more energy.

Obstructions or unloaded scan cells stop generation without force-loading terrain.
