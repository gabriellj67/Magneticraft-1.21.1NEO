# Gasification Unit

The gasification unit converts accepted solid inputs into recipe-defined gases after reaching the required temperature. Input, output, fluid tank, and heat state persist across saves.

Concrete recipes are in the `magneticraft:gasification_unit` recipe family and appear in JEI when installed. The process pauses if heat is too low or the output tank cannot accept the full result.

Use the machine page for item and fluid automation sides; simulated transfers never mutate the tank.
