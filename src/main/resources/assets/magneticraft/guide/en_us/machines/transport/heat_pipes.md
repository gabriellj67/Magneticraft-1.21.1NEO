# Heat Pipes

Heat pipes connect compatible thermal nodes and exchange heat within the dedicated thermal domain. Ordinary pipes lose more heat to the environment; insulated pipes reduce that loss.

Heat sinks deliberately dissipate network heat and can protect a process from excessive temperature. Use a thermometer on each relevant face to find disconnected or cold nodes.

Topology and energy transfer are server authoritative. Unloaded nodes pause and no pipe force-loads its neighboring chunks.
