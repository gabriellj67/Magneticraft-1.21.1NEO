# Utilities

Utilities provide storage, water, automation, environmental control, and crafting support. Each device follows the side and slot rules shown in its in-game machine page.

The shelving unit and shipping container are formed storage structures. The water generator, airlock, feeding trough, small tank, wooden crate, and fabricator are single-block devices.

Creative administration devices such as the infinite energy source are intentionally obtainable only where their game mode permits; internal structure gaps and moving placeholders are never public content.
