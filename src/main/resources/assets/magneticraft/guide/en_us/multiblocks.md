# Multiblocks

Place a controller, face it in the intended direction, and reproduce every layer shown in the in-game structure view. Structure members are owned by one controller; overlapping claims are rejected.

Formation and removal are server authoritative. A structure pauses while required chunks are unloaded and resumes after validation; it never force-loads chunks or treats unloading as dismantling.

Controller pages list item capacity, electrical and heat requirements, and every fluid tank. Use only the declared ports to avoid blocked inputs or outputs.

See [Nuclear Power](nuclear_power.md) for reactor and spent-fuel-pool sizes, ports, and safe operation.
