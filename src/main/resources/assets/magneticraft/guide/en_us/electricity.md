# Electricity

Magneticraft electricity models voltage, current, resistance, and stored joules. Network topology changes are processed on the server, and stable networks reuse their graph instead of rebuilding every tick.

Use cables for adjacent blocks and wire endpoints for long distances. Forge Energy does not replace this domain; transformer and heater machines provide explicit bridges at `1 J = 1 FE`.

Read [Fundamentals](electricity/fundamentals.md), [Conversion Rates](electricity/conversion_rates.md), and [Transportation](electricity/transportation.md).
