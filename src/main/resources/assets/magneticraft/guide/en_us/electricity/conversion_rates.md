# Conversion Rates

Magneticraft fixes the adapter rate at `1 joule = 1 Forge Energy`. No hidden multiplier is applied at FE bridges, portable tools, or machine buffers.

Transfer rate is still bounded by each bridge and storage device. A one-to-one unit ratio therefore does not imply unlimited FE/t. Voltage thresholds can also pause a bridge even when energy exists on one side.

Use machine pages and Jade, when installed, to inspect stored energy; use the voltmeter for native electrical values.
